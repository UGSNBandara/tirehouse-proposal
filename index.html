What it covers

Seven modules, one stock truth

Tyres, oil and batteries keep their own details but share the same stock, GRN and billing flow.

Stock

Items & stock

Tyres by size, brand and DOT week. Oil by grade and pack size, including drums sold by the litre. Batteries by model, Ah and warranty.

Receiving

GRN

Receive against a purchase order, flag short or damaged lines, then post to stock.

Selling

Issues & bills

Quotations, invoices, job issues, returns and old-battery trade-in. Receipts print straight from the app.

Money

Store accounts

Cash, card, cheque and credit sales. Customer receivables, supplier payables, expenses, daily cash book.

Control

Members & approvals

Manager sets what each staff member may do. Restricted actions wait for the manager's phone.

Live

Manager view

Sales, cash, low stock and pending approvals on the dashboard and on the manager's phone.

Messages

SMS

Invoice receipts, payment reminders, warranty and service-due notices, and approval codes.

Who uses it

Two roles, access per member

No long list of job titles. Everyone is either the Manager or a Staff member, and the Manager switches individual permissions on or off for each staff member.

Manager

Owner and anyone the owner trusts fully
  • Sees everything, including cost prices, profit and accounts
  • Adds staff and sets each person's access
  • Approves discounts, voids and adjustments from the phone or the desktop app
  • Gets live alerts and SMS

Staff member

Counter, store and helpers
  • Signs in with their own name and PIN, so every bill shows who made it
  • Does only what the Manager has switched on for them
  • Cannot see cost prices or accounts unless allowed
  • Asks the Manager when a restricted action comes up

Manager phone · UI concept

The shop in the manager's pocket

For the Manager only. Read the live numbers, decide approvals and look up stock without going to the shop.

Authorised actions

How the manager approves from a device

A staff member cannot finish a restricted action alone. The system holds it, asks the Manager, and records the answer.

Staff asksA staff member tries something outside their access, such as a 12% discount.
System holdsThe action is saved as pending. Nothing posts to stock or accounts yet.
Phone alertsPush notification goes to the Manager. SMS follows if the app is offline.
Manager decidesApprove with fingerprint or PIN on a registered device, or reject with a note.
Counter updatesThe bill unlocks on the desktop app and the decision is recorded with who, when and from which device.

The Manager registers their phone once. The app asks for fingerprint, face or PIN on every approval, and an expired request cannot be approved. Without data, the system sends a one-time SMS code the Manager can read out to the counter.

How it is built

Desktop app at the shop, backend in the cloud

We host only the backend and database. Everything else runs on your own computers and the Manager's phone.

At your shop

Desktop app

  • Windows app on each counter and back-office PC
  • Receipt printer and barcode scanner
  • Local copy of recent data for short outages
⇄
We host this

Backend

  • API and PostgreSQL database
  • Approval rules and audit log
  • Live updates to every device
  • Nightly backups
→
Reaches the manager

Phone and SMS

  • Manager app or installed web app
  • Push notifications
  • SMS gateway for customers and codes

If the internet drops OFFLINE

The desktop app keeps selling and printing, and queues the bills. When the connection returns it sends them to the backend. Approvals and live phone updates wait until then.

Your data OWNERSHIP

The database is yours. You can request an export any time, and backups are stored in a separate place from the live database.

Quotation · Sri Lankan rupees

Three plans, one monthly charge

A fixed one-time price, a small monthly charge for hosting and support, and SMS billed separately by the number actually sent.

SMS charges

Depends on how many SMS are sent

SMS is not part of any plan price or monthly charge. It is counted from the system's own SMS log and billed at the end of each month, at the rate of the SMS provider. A message longer than 160 characters counts as more than one SMS. The rate is confirmed when we choose the provider.
